Brand guidelines

Branding

Tidewell is a small-batch roaster on a cold coastline. The brand should feel calm, crafted and a little warm, like the first cup after a morning on the water.

Color story

Deep tide navy and sea-glass teal carry the calm and the craft. Sand adds the warmth of the cup and the shore. Ember orange is the single loud note, kept for the actions we want people to take.

Logo use

The mark is two waves in a circle, the wordmark is set in Georgia with COFFEE tracked out in Ember. Use the primary logo on light backgrounds, the reversed logo on Navy, and the mark alone where space is tight.

Minimum size

Primary and reversed: 120px wide on screen. Mark alone: 24px.

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side.

Please don't

Stretch or squash it
Recolor it
Rotate it
Place it on low-contrast backgrounds
